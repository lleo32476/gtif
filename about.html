About GTIF

Georgetown's student-run technology investment fund focused on valuation, innovation, and career development.

Our Story

Who We Are

The Georgetown Technology Investment Fund is a student-led investment organization dedicated to developing the next generation of technology investors. GTIF provides Georgetown students with a rigorous, hands-on learning environment focused on analyzing, valuing, and understanding technology-driven businesses across public and private markets.

Mission

To equip Georgetown undergraduate students with world-class valuation skills, industry connections, and career placement opportunities in technology investing.

Vision

To become the nation's premier undergraduate student-run technology investment fund, bridging the gap between Georgetown University and Silicon Valley.

Our Purpose

Why GTIF Exists

GTIF was founded to address a clear gap in Georgetown's student investment landscape. While Georgetown has several strong investment organizations, most provide broad generalist coverage or focus on other specialized areas. GTIF offers a dedicated platform for students interested in technology investing, giving members the opportunity to develop deeper expertise in the industries shaping the future of public and private markets.

The Case for Tech

Why Technology Investing

Technology is one of the most important forces shaping the modern economy. From software and artificial intelligence to semiconductors, fintech, healthcare technology, and digital platforms, technology is now embedded across nearly every major industry. Because these companies often rely on intangible assets, recurring revenue models, research and development, network effects, and long-term scalability, they require a specialized valuation approach. GTIF gives students the tools to understand these businesses at a deeper level.

How We Operate

Training & Investment Process

Phase 01

Foundations

Accounting, valuation, financial modeling, and technology business model basics.

Phase 02

Sector Deep Dives

Coverage across software, AI, semiconductors, fintech, and tech-enabled services.

Phase 03

Stock Pitch

Teams prepare formal investment recommendations presented to the full fund.

Phase 04

Simulated Portfolio

Approved ideas tracked in a live portfolio, with real AUM as a future goal.

GTIF's analyst program is designed to turn interest into real investment skill. New members learn accounting, valuation, financial modeling, technology business models, and investment research before working in teams to prepare formal stock pitches. Approved investment ideas will be tracked through a simulated portfolio, allowing members to experience the responsibilities of a real investment fund while keeping the organization focused on education, discipline, and long-term development.

Our Culture

Community & Culture

At its core, GTIF is more than an investment club. It is a mentorship-driven community built around curiosity, ambition, collaboration, and applied learning. Members work closely with peers, receive guidance from experienced student leaders, present professional-quality investment ideas, and build the confidence needed to pursue competitive careers in finance and technology.
