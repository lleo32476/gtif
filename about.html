Our Story

Who We Are

The Georgetown Technology Investment Fund is a student-led investment organization dedicated to developing the next generation of technology investors. GTIF gives Georgetown students a rigorous, hands-on environment for analyzing, valuing, and understanding technology-driven businesses across public and private markets.

Mission

To equip Georgetown undergraduates with world-class valuation skills, industry connections, and career placement in technology investing.

Vision

To become the nation's premier undergraduate technology investment fund, bridging Georgetown and Silicon Valley.

Our Purpose

Why GTIF Exists

GTIF was founded to fill a clear gap in Georgetown's student investment landscape. Most existing organizations provide broad generalist coverage or focus on other specialized areas. GTIF is a dedicated platform for students serious about technology investing — a place to build deeper expertise in the industries defining the future of markets.

The Case for Tech

Why Technology Investing

Technology is the defining force of the modern economy. From software and artificial intelligence to semiconductors, fintech, and digital platforms, it is now embedded across nearly every major industry.

These businesses run on intangible assets, recurring revenue, research and development, and network effects — and that demands a specialized valuation approach. GTIF gives students the tools to understand them at a deeper level.

Activities

What We Do

Investment Research

Analyst Training

A structured curriculum in accounting, financial modeling, and valuation built for tech business models.

Stock Pitches

How We Operate

Training & Investment Process

Phase 01

Foundations

Accounting, valuation, financial modeling, and technology business model basics.

Phase 02

Sector Deep Dives

Coverage across software, AI, semiconductors, fintech, and tech-enabled services.

Phase 03

Stock Pitch

Teams prepare formal investment recommendations presented to the full fund.

Phase 04

Simulated Portfolio

Approved ideas tracked in a live portfolio, with real AUM as a future goal.

GTIF's analyst program turns interest into real investment skill. New members learn accounting, valuation, financial modeling, and technology business models before working in teams to prepare formal stock pitches. Approved ideas are tracked through a simulated portfolio, giving members the responsibilities of a real fund while keeping the organization focused on education, discipline, and long-term development.

Our Culture

Community & Culture

At its core, GTIF is more than an investment club. It is a mentorship-driven community built on curiosity, ambition, and applied learning. Members work closely with peers, present professional-quality investment ideas, and build the confidence to pursue competitive careers in finance and technology.
